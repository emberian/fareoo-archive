  > §32, the final chapter, returns to inheritance and open recursion between methods, and finally introduces the real thing in the very last formal section, §32.9, but without elaborating on the issues it raises. §32.10 is a collection of citations, some good, some OK, some bad (including plenty of bad Cardelli papers), and notably omitting Eifrig.
