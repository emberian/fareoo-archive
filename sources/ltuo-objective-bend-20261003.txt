1. Open extensions before closed objects

READ: 5.4.1-4, 8.2.1-4, 8.4.1.3-8.4.4.
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.M.E%29
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.Recursively_.Constrained_.Types%29

Context C, inherited target V and result W vary independently: V→C→W in the
book's super-first convention. Heterogeneous extensions form a category for
fixed C; C=V=W is a useful closed special case, not the general construction.
Targets include functions, numbers, nested specifications and type descriptors.

PROPOSED self-first convention: Ext C V W=C→V→W, with
compose parent child self base=child self (parent self base).
All ancestors see ONE eventual self; super denotes the whole inherited target.
Source annotations must retain the three types and an explicit order convention.

A value-dependent family V self over a fixed C does not yet provide modular
future-self typing. Separately compiled extensions must quantify over future
self/super TYPES satisfying requirements while preserving unknown row members.
Self-dependent associated types, binary methods and nested families test this.
The book supplies proposals, not a checked complete decidable type theory: choose
an annotated checking fragment and prove its own metatheory. Ember explicitly
permits changing Bend's types/core; existing Data rules are not a source ceiling.

2. Operational lazy construction, distinct from equations

READ: 5.3.2.3-5, 5.5.4, 10.3.2; original poof section 3 and EOOMI section 5.2.
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.Lazy_.Y%29
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.S.Ro.Ro.S%29

Deferral applies to arbitrary computations, not just functions; sharing applies
per allocated thunk, not automatically to every equal function application.
Whole-record suspension and individually suspended fields are different.
An equation x=f(x) alone is not successful construction: every value solves
x=id(x), while operational fix(id) has no weak-head result.

PROPOSED: independent weak-head CBN source meaning; call-by-need graph executor
with suspended/evaluating/cached cells and update frames, common self pointer,
and lazy fields. Prove result adequacy separately from sharing and costs. Relate
cyclic heaps by a graph relation, not naïve finite textual expansion. Capacity
exhaustion is resumable suspension. An observable blackhole error needs an
explicit source/refusal contract, not an unproved claim of divergence.

Clocked CPS can implement lazy semantics if it represents delayed expressions,
captures and forcing faithfully. Clocking a strict interpreter does not suffice:
keep(7,fix(identity)) and {good=7,bad=self.bad}.good must both return 7. A Data/table
backend can represent closures and generic targets, but needs an embedding and
adequacy proof; the representation does not require Data-only source captures.

3. First-class incomplete specifications and reflection

READ: 6.1.2.3-6.1.4.
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.First_.Issue__.Incomplete_.Specifications%29
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.R.C%29

An incomplete or divergent target must not prevent inspecting and extending its
specification. Reflection through recursive self must still recover that spec.
PROPOSED: keep generic computational Fix; explicitly construct a prototype with
specification plus lazy target (and declared metadata), by tying self around the
wrapper INSIDE the fixed point. Do not redefine every generic fix as an object.
A descriptor containing selector names alone is not the extension computation.
Reflection must name which syntax, identities, code/environment or semantic
operations are observable; optimization equivalence depends on that boundary.

Book 10.6 is mostly an outline and 10.6.1 an empty heading, not a completed MOP.
Cross-check actual reflection work in First-Class Implementations and Climbing:
https://fare.tunes.org/files/fci2017/fci.html
https://fare.tunes.org/files/climbing/climbing.html

4. Laws, proofs, ownership and authority are separate

READ: 8.4.3, 9.1.6, 9.2.9.
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.Inherited_.Interfaces_vs_.Orthogonal_.Protocols%29

General extension may be nonmonotonic. Retaining every old law is one chosen
compatible-interface policy, not a universal consequence of inheritance.
PROPOSED: unrestricted first-class construction; explicit retained contracts
with actual provider evidence; separate current authority to publish/adopt/run.
Allow after-the-fact protocols in a scoped implementation environment rather
than requiring shared class ancestry or ungoverned ambient mutation.

Shared closures need transitively shareable captures/results; immutable wrappers
must not hide affine obligations. Resource addresses being copyable data does
not establish resource ownership. Suspension/checkpoint must conserve ownership;
no duplication and terminal discharge are safety obligations, while eventual
consumption of a suspended/diverging computation needs additional liveness.
Pure fields may describe actions; executing an effect while forcing a shared
field requires an explicit sharing-sensitive effect semantics.

Keep unrestricted recursion out of total proof construction and definitional
conversion. This does NOT forbid total logic from proving equations, contextual
equivalence, simulations or safety about partial program syntax. A finite trace
is useful evidence of termination, not the only meaningful law about recursion.

5. C4 and suffixes require the right input structure

READ: 7.3.3, all 7.3.5 constraints, 7.4.2-6, 10.4.2; accompanying pommette.scm
c4-linearize and its tests (read, not executed).
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.Shape_.Determinism__.Consistency_across_.Equivalent_.Ancestries%29
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.Suffix_.Specifications%29

Suffix means contiguous TAIL of descendants' precedence lists, stronger than
subsequence monotonicity; incompatible suffix ancestry must be rejected. Explicit
mix(E,E) applies twice, while one generative DAG identity shared by a diamond
appears once. Equal code/captures do not automatically identify DAG nodes.

The book's bare-DAG shape-determinism wording is too strong. Its implementation
maps ((C A)(C B)) to C A B O and reversed presentation to C B A O, despite the
same partial order. Deterministic equivariance under the swap automorphism of a
symmetric bare poset cannot choose one strict total order. PROPOSED theorem:
renaming/isomorphism invariance of the ORDERED presentation, including local
parent sequences and suffix marks. Do not advertise canonical order from a bare
DAG. Some stronger heuristic claims in the book are explicitly conjectural.
Nested-focus optimization must preserve inherited ordering even for ancestors
whose own extension does not modify that focus (9.1.6.4).

6. Persistence is of continuing computation

READ: LTUO 6.3.2; First-Class Implementations 2-5, Climbing 2-5; Persistence Model
execution contexts, domains, synchronization, atomicity and schema upgrade.
https://fare.tunes.org/files/cs/poof/ltuo.html#%28part._.Mo.Ia.C.U%29
https://github.com/mighty-gerbils/gerbil-persist/blob/master/persist.md

PROPOSED: preserve heap, aliases/cache, control/frames, captures, code revisions,
effect journal, ownership and fencing. Distinguish stable object identity, code
revision, pinned in-flight invocation and upgrade policy. A serialized Data
residual is not inherently safe to migrate: the same continuation tag may now
interpret an old cents payload as dollars, or replay an uncertain external act.
A total old→new field converter does not relate continuation meaning.

Migration needs observe-old→abstract-continuation→realize-new plus effect/resource
correspondence. Quiescence is one valid policy; transferring complete uncertain
recovery state with fencing is another if proved. From arbitrary reachable
interruptions, observation itself needs bounded/nonblocking progress to an
abstract safe state. Forward stuttering simulation alone proves none of this.
The runtime owner reports current carrier evolution uses a new revision/instance
with old behavior pinned; that is narrower than same-identity live-frame migration.

7. What to prove and consume next

* Elaboration convergence correspondence under supported source observations,
  including heterogeneous targets, final self, whole super and captured closures.
* Operational lazy adequacy, sharing/update invariant, and progress for RUNNING
  states; separate initialization, terminal correspondence, refusal honesty and
  completeness. Rank decrease cannot be demanded of terminal self-loop states.
* Approximation adequacy through ground observations or related higher-order
  values. Exact syntactic WHNF equality is too strong: fix(λs.λb.λx.s) yields a
  closure containing fix, while finite approximants contain finite unrollings.
* Future-row typing and contract substitution, with closed-object discharge of
  assumptions about final self; no total-core consistency transferred silently.
* Ordered C4/suffix soundness, diagnostics and generative identity semantics.
* Checkpoint/resume and backend migration from reachable interruptions, preserving
  continuation, cache aliases, ownership and visible effect history.
